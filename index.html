How It All Started

October 31, 2022 — the day everything changed.

On Halloween 2022, the brave men and women of South Hays Fire Department saved our family member David's life. Words couldn't express the gratitude we felt.

So we picked up our laser engraver and let the wood do the talking. We created custom boards for each of the angels who saved him — their names, their truck, their badge — permanently engraved as a thank-you that would last forever.

From that moment, SS Laser Engraving & More was born. Every board we make carries that same heart — whether it's a wedding gift, a holiday board, or a memorial for a beloved pet.
